# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume they are acting in good faith. Criticise code, not
the person who wrote it.

## Expected behaviour

- Keep discussion focused on the technical question at hand.
- Accept that other people have different levels of experience. A question that
  seems obvious to you was not obvious to you once either.
- Give feedback that someone can act on. "This is wrong" helps nobody; "this
  treats an RPC error as an empty result, which hides failures" does.
- Accept a decision you disagree with once it has been made and explained.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Discriminatory language or imagery, including anything targeting a person's
  background, identity, beliefs, or appearance.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or deliberately derailing issues.
- Using this project to promote scams, phishing, or malicious contracts. Given
  what this software does, this is taken seriously.

## Scope

This applies to everything in this project's spaces — issues, pull requests,
commit messages, discussions — and to any place where someone is representing
the project publicly.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately via [Telegram](https://t.me/mckellen_eth).
Reports are handled confidentially. Please include enough detail to understand
what happened — links to the relevant issue or comment are ideal.

## Enforcement

The maintainer will review every report and respond in a way that fits the
situation: a private explanation, a request for a public apology, edited or
removed content, or a temporary or permanent ban from the project's spaces.

Enforcement decisions are the maintainer's to make. If you believe a decision
about you was unfair, you can say so through the same private channel.
